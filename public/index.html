Student Petition Against Unfair Bus Fees

SNM Institute of Management and Technology (SNMIMT), Maliankara [INDEX 1.2.1]

TO: The Principal, SNMIMT College Management, APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), and Respected Legal/Police Authorities.

We, the undersigned students of SNMIMT, formally raise this grievance regarding the sudden demand for college bus transportation fees. At the time of our admission, the institution explicitly offered and promoted Free Transportation for the entire 4-year academic program as documented in institutional brochures and admission notices. Asking us to pay transportation fees midway violates the commitment made to students and parents. We request immediate intervention to restore free transportation as originally offered.
